## SECTION ONE — PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Framework governs the relationship between DGENIE and every parent or guardian of an enrolled learner, applying equally to Home Track and Membership Track families, and covering the entire learner journey, from enrollment through Legacy Bearer standing on the Lifelong Membership Journey established in Blueprint 06.

This Framework does not replace day-to-day family life. It establishes the constitutional expectations that make the partnership between family and institution real rather than aspirational.

---

## SECTION TWO — FOUNDATIONAL PARENT PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLES

Parents are a child's first and most influential educators. DGENIE strengthens the family; it never replaces it, per Blueprint 01's Principle Ten: "The home and the school share responsibility for raising children. Effective education requires intentional collaboration between both."

The school and family work from a shared vision, not competing ones. Partnership is built on trust, transparency, and mutual respect. Parents hold the right to be informed and involved in their child's formation. DGENIE respects a family's cultural, spiritual, and disciplinary values, consistent with Blueprint 07's principle of respect for family values. Communication is proactive, clear, and age-appropriate. Parents are contributors to the community established in Blueprint 06, not merely consumers of a service DGENIE provides.

---

## SECTION THREE — PARENT RIGHTS

Extending the transparency and involvement commitments already established in Blueprint 07, parents hold the right to transparent access to their child's Genius Level Profile and Genius Portfolio, per Blueprint 08; regular communication with their child's Mentor and Teachers; a clear understanding of how My Genie works and what it remembers about their child, per Blueprint 02 and Blueprint 13; to be heard in any decision materially affecting their child, per Blueprint 07's collaborative decision-making principle; to raise concerns without retaliation, per Blueprint 07 and Blueprint 09; to withdraw their child from DGENIE at any time, with appropriate transition support; access to parent education and support resources, per Section Eight; to participate in the community life of DGENIE, including their Local Learning Circle and its events; to be informed immediately of any safeguarding concern involving their child, per Blueprint 07; to receive clear, transparent information about fees, policies, and expectations; and to participate in the DGENIE Parent Community Council at local, regional, and global levels, per Section Eleven.

---

## SECTION FOUR — PARENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Parents are expected to partner with DGENIE in their child's formation and education, not to delegate it entirely; to support their child's participation in the Genius Cycle and Signature Experiences established in Blueprint 04; to communicate openly and honestly with their child's Mentor and Teachers; to respect the Community Standards established in Blueprint 06 and Blueprint 07; to participate in their Local Learning Circle where reasonably possible; to engage in regular check-ins with their child's Mentor; to support their child's reflection and portfolio development at home; to model the values DGENIE seeks to cultivate, including integrity, respect, and compassion; and to honour the partnership by raising concerns directly and constructively rather than allowing them to go unspoken.

This partnership carries obligations in both directions. The rights in Section Three and the responsibilities in this Section are not separable; a family that expects the full benefit of partnership without engaging its responsibilities, or an institution that expects family cooperation without honouring these rights, has broken the partnership this Framework exists to protect.

## SECTION FIVE — PARENT ONBOARDING AND WELCOME

__Pre-enrollment__ includes an information session explaining DGENIE's Vision, Methodology, and constitutional commitments, so that a family's decision to enroll is genuinely informed.

__Enrollment__ includes a formal agreement to this Framework and to the Community Standards established in Blueprint 06. __Added 2026-08-15, closing Finding 6.3 from this session's structural audit:__ enrollment is also where a family makes the specific choice Blueprint 03's Spiritual Formation Dimension names directly, whether their child is exposed to that Dimension's Christian-specific content and practice or to its non-doctrinal alternative. This is recorded as its own distinct choice, not inferred from the general agreement above, and can be revisited at any later Portfolio Review at the family's request. __Added 2026-08-15, same day:__ a family choosing the Christian-specific version of Spiritual Formation may also request a Mentor with a shared faith background, a distinct, related choice recorded alongside it rather than assumed by it, honored at first assignment and at any later rotation or renewal under Blueprint 11 Section Four where caseload availability allows; a family that has not made this request, or has chosen the non-doctrinal alternative, is assigned the same way every other Mentor assignment already works, without faith background as a factor.

__Orientation__ introduces the Genius Cycle, My Genie, the Genius Portfolio, and the family's Local Learning Circle.

__The first thirty days__ include a Mentor introduction, an initial parent-teacher conference, and a clear explanation of the communication channels described in Section Six.

__The Parent Companion.__ Parents are introduced to their own Parent Companion agent, the AI system named in Blueprint 08 and specified in Blueprint 14, built specifically to help a parent understand their child's Portfolio and formation, never to make parenting decisions on their behalf.

## SECTION SIX — COMMUNICATION CHANNELS

__Mentor check-ins__ follow the cadence established in Blueprint 04, weekly on the Home Track and at every biweekly session on the Membership Track, giving parents on each track a communication rhythm that matches their family's actual engagement with DGENIE.

__Portfolio reviews__ occur termly on the Home Track and quarterly on the Membership Track, per Blueprint 04, with parent participation built into the review itself, not offered only as a summary afterward.

__Teacher conferences__ are scheduled directly with subject teachers, per the Teacher & Mentor Framework established in Blueprint 11.

__Community communication__ flows through Circle-level announcements, Regional gatherings, and Global community updates, per Blueprint 06.

__Emergency communication__ provides immediate notification of any safeguarding concern or significant incident, per Blueprint 07, without delay for either track.

__The Parent Portal__ is a digital dashboard giving parents access to their child's Portfolio, schedule, and communications in one place, consistent with the transparency this Framework requires throughout.

---

## SECTION SEVEN — PARENT PARTICIPATION IN FORMATION

Parents participate in their child's formation through reflection support at home, per Blueprint 04; service learning participation, per Blueprint 04; Local Learning Circle activities, per Blueprint 06; Community Impact Projects and Signature Experience support, per Blueprint 04 and Blueprint 05.

Parents receive guidance on supporting their child's Genius Discovery at home, and are invited to contribute their own observations directly to the Genius Portfolio, an entry type already provided for in Blueprint 08's Information Architecture. Parents are partners in character formation, reinforcing at home the same values taught within DGENIE rather than leaving formation to feel like something that happens only at school.

---

## SECTION EIGHT — PARENT EDUCATION AND SUPPORT

DGENIE provides resources helping parents understand the DGENIE educational philosophy and methodology; the Genius Level system and its assessment approach; the role of AI and My Genie in their child's education, per Blueprint 07's transparency principle; safeguarding and digital wellbeing, per Blueprint 07; and how to support their child's learning at home.

This education is offered through online resources and workshops; Local Learning Circle gatherings; one-on-one Mentor conversations; and the Parent Companion agent introduced in Section Five.

---

## SECTION NINE — PARENT FEEDBACK AND VOICE

Parents hold formal channels to provide feedback on their child's educational experience; Mentor and Teacher performance; community life; and DGENIE's own policies and practices.

Feedback is collected regularly through surveys and community forums, reviewed by the Chief Community Officer, and reported to the Board of Stewards. Parents may join Advisory Councils, per Blueprint 08, and the formal structure described in Section Eleven, to provide ongoing input beyond periodic surveys.

---

## SECTION TEN — CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND CONCERNS

Parents are encouraged to raise concerns directly and constructively. Concerns are addressed through the Responding to Concerns principles established in Blueprint 07.

A parent whose concern is not resolved through standard channels holds the right to escalate to the Oversight Body established in Blueprint 09. Parents are protected from retaliation for raising any concern in good faith, per Blueprint 07 and Blueprint 09.

## SECTION ELEVEN — THE DGENIE PARENT COMMUNITY COUNCIL

Extending Blueprint 06 and the Advisory Councils named in Blueprint 08, a formal parent participation structure exists to give parents a collective voice in the life of the community. This structure is advisory, not governing; it informs decision-making but holds no constitutional authority, per the Separation of Authority established in Blueprint 08.

__Restructured 2026-08-14, closing a duplication this session's structural audit identified: this Section previously ran its own three-layer Regional and Global Parent Assembly structure, elected representatives and all, alongside Blueprint 08's separately-defined Advisory Councils, which serve the identical function, advisory-only community and family input, with the identical authority, none. Regional and global parent voice now flows through Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils directly, ending the parallel structure rather than running two elected bodies with the same job. This is a consolidation, not a reduction in parent voice: nothing about how much say parents have changes, only how many separately-constituted bodies exist to carry it.__

__Eleven.One — Name and Purpose.__ The local layer of parent participation is the Circle Parent Forum. Its purpose is to give parents a formal, constructive voice in the community; to strengthen partnership between families and DGENIE; to provide feedback on programs, policies, and community life; to coordinate parent volunteer efforts; to support Local Learning Circle activities; and to serve as a communication bridge between families and leadership. Beyond the Circle level, that same voice is carried forward through Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils rather than through a second, parent-specific structure.

__Eleven.Two — Layers of Participation.__ At the __Local level,__ the Circle Parent Forum includes every parent within a Local Learning Circle as a member, meets quarterly or as needed, is convened by the Community Mentor, and focuses on local concerns, Circle activities, service projects, and parent education. Where regional or global parent input is needed, a Circle Parent Forum puts parent representation forward into Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils, the same channel every other form of community and family input already uses at that level, rather than into a parent-only Regional or Global Assembly that no longer exists as a separate structure. __A gap named honestly, not solved by this consolidation:__ Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils entry does not itself specify an appointment or election process any more than the structure this Section previously ran did; this consolidation removes a duplicate structure, it does not by itself answer how a parent representative is actually selected. That remains an open item, named in Blueprint 08 Part One, Section Three.

__Eleven.Three — Authority and Limits.__ Parent participation, at every level, is advisory, not governing. It holds no constitutional authority or decision-making power, per Blueprint 08. Parents may make recommendations to the Chief Community Officer, the Board of Stewards, and, through Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils, the Constitutional Council, and every recommendation is received, considered, and answered in writing. No parent body holds authority over curriculum, formation, hiring, or safeguarding policy. Safeguarding concerns are escalated exclusively through Blueprint 07's channels, never through a parent forum or council.

__Eleven.Four — Relationship with the Board of Stewards.__ The Board receives parent input through Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils, may invite parent representatives to attend, without vote, meetings relevant to family matters, and treats parent feedback as a formal input to its decision-making, never as binding authority.

__Eleven.Five — Relationship with the Chief Community Officer.__ The Chief Community Officer is the Circle Parent Forum's primary liaison and responds to its recommendations within sixty days; regional and global parent input is convened through Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils rather than through a separate parent-specific convening process.

__Eleven.Six — Safeguarding and Confidentiality.__ Parent Councils operate under the same Community Standards as every member, per Blueprint 07. Confidential learner information is never discussed in a parent forum, per Blueprint 08. Safeguarding concerns are escalated through the crisis principles in Blueprint 07, never raised or resolved through a parent forum.

__Eleven.Seven — Volunteer Coordination.__ Parent Councils coordinate volunteer efforts at the Circle level. Every volunteer is subject to the standards established in Blueprint 07, and volunteer service is recognized in community life, per Blueprint 06's Recognition Night.

__Eleven.Eight — Implementation.__ Local Circle Parent Forums are established immediately upon the formation of any Local Learning Circle, with a transition plan provided for any existing informal parent group to align with this constitutional structure. Regional and global parent representation into Blueprint 08's Advisory Councils is established as the community grows, at the discretion of the Chief Community Officer and the Board of Stewards, rather than through a separate Regional or Global Assembly standing up its own structure; this growth is the direct trigger for, and is governed jointly with, the expansion process established in Blueprint 10.

---

## SECTION TWELVE — PARENT ROLE IN THE COMMUNITY

Parents are members of their Local Learning Circle, per Blueprint 06. Parents may volunteer in community roles, per Blueprint 06. Parents may serve on Advisory Councils, per Blueprint 08. Parents act as ambassadors of the DGENIE community, extending its mission through their own networks, and model the community's values for their children in a way no formal instruction alone could replicate.

---

## SECTION THIRTEEN — SAFEGUARDING INTEGRATION

Parents are partners in safeguarding, per Blueprint 07, not observers of a process DGENIE carries out alone. Parents are informed of safeguarding policies and their own responsibilities under them. Parents are notified immediately of any safeguarding concern involving their child. Parents hold the right to know who their child interacts with in DGENIE spaces. Parents are encouraged to raise any safeguarding concern immediately, without waiting for a scheduled communication.

This is not a separate obligation appended to the Framework; it is present in the communication channels of Section Six, the participation structures of Section Eleven, and the conflict resolution process of Section Ten, since a partnership that only remembers safeguarding when something has already gone wrong has not genuinely integrated it.

---

## SECTION FOURTEEN — MONITORING AND REVIEW

This Framework is reviewed annually by the Chief Community Officer. Parent satisfaction is measured and tracked over time. The Oversight Body established in Blueprint 09 may audit parent partnership practices as part of its Constitutional Audit. Feedback from parents, gathered per Section Nine, is used to improve this Framework rather than treating it as fixed once adopted.

---

## SECTION FIFTEEN — AMENDMENT OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Amendments to this Framework require a proposal from the Chief Community Officer and approval by the Board of Stewards, applying the Framework Amendments tier of the Amendment Process established in Blueprint 08.

---

## CONSTITUTIONAL DEFINITIONS

__Parent Community Council.__ The three-layer advisory structure, Circle Parent Forum, Regional Parent Assembly, and Global Parent Assembly, established in Section Eleven to give parents a collective voice without constitutional authority.

__Circle Parent Forum.__ The local layer of the Parent Community Council, comprising every parent within a Local Learning Circle, per Section Eleven.Two.

__Regional Parent Assembly.__ The regional layer of the Parent Community Council, comprising elected representatives from each Circle in a region, per Section Eleven.Two.

__Global Parent Assembly.__ The global layer of the Parent Community Council, comprising elected representatives from each region, meeting alongside the Global Gathering, per Section Eleven.Two.

__Parent Companion.__ The AI agent named in Blueprint 08 and specified in Blueprint 14, built to help a parent understand their child's Portfolio and formation, never to make a parenting decision on their behalf.

__Parent Portal.__ The digital dashboard giving parents access to their child's Portfolio, schedule, and communications, per Section Six.

__Collaborative Decision-Making.__ The principle, established in Blueprint 07, that a parent participates in any decision materially affecting their child rather than being merely informed of it afterward.
