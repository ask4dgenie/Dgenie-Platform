__PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT__

The DGENIE Formation Framework establishes the philosophy, principles, and processes through which DGENIE intentionally forms the whole person.

Education is not merely the transfer of knowledge.

Education is the formation of human beings.

DGENIE therefore commits itself to the intentional development of every learner's spiritual life, character, intellect, emotions, relationships, creativity, leadership, and sense of purpose.

Every educational experience within DGENIE shall answer one fundamental question:

__Who is this learner becoming?__

__THE DGENIE PRINCIPLE OF FORMATION__

DGENIE believes that every educational system forms people.

The only question is whether that formation is intentional or accidental.

We reject education that merely fills minds with information while neglecting wisdom, character, and purpose.

True education shapes the whole person.

__THE PURPOSE OF FORMATION__

The purpose of formation is to help every learner become the fullest expression of the person God created them to be.

Formation is therefore concerned not only with what students know, but also:

- Who they become.
- What they believe.
- How they think.
- How they live.
- How they serve.
- How they lead.
- How they influence the world.

__THE SEVEN DIMENSIONS OF DGENIE FORMATION__

Every learner shall be intentionally formed in seven interconnected dimensions.

__DIMENSION ONE__

__Spiritual Formation__

Spiritual Formation is the foundation of every other dimension.

Students are guided to:

- Know God personally.
- Develop a biblical worldview.
- Love truth.
- Practice prayer.
- Study Scripture.
- Worship sincerely.
- Walk in obedience.
- Discern God's direction.
- Depend upon the Holy Spirit.
- Live as faithful disciples of Christ.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate whose relationship with God shapes every area of life.

__A parent's choice, named directly, added 2026-08-15, closing Finding 6.3 from this session's structural audit.__ DGENIE's own institutional frame is explicitly Christian, stated above, throughout this Framework, and in Blueprint 01's Graduate Profile, and that identity does not change per family; it is a statement of what DGENIE itself is, not a claim about what every enrolled family must themselves believe or practice. Whether a specific learner is exposed to this Dimension's Christian-specific content and practice, knowing God personally, a biblical worldview, prayer, Scripture study, worship, discerning God's direction, dependence on the Holy Spirit, discipleship of Christ, is the enrolling parent's choice, made explicitly at enrollment per Blueprint 12, never assumed by default from enrollment alone.

Where a parent does not choose Christian-specific content for their child, this Dimension is not left empty. It continues under a general frame instead: the pursuit of truth, examined conviction, and transcendent purpose and meaning-making, formed with the same seriousness this Framework gives every other dimension, without content specific to Christianity or any other particular tradition. That learner's desired outcome becomes a graduate whose sense of purpose and considered conviction shapes every area of life, the same structural role this Dimension plays for every learner, filled with different content by family choice rather than removed.

This choice affects this Dimension alone. The other six dimensions of Formation below apply to every learner identically regardless of it, since none of them requires content specific to a religious tradition. A Mentor's own hiring qualification, including the theological grounding Blueprint 11 requires of every Mentor, is unchanged by this: that qualification is what makes a Mentor capable of delivering either version of this Dimension well; which version a specific learner actually receives is set by that learner's family, not by the Mentor's own formation.

__DIMENSION TWO__

__Character Formation__

Character determines how gifts are used.

DGENIE intentionally cultivates:

- Integrity
- Honesty
- Responsibility
- Humility
- Courage
- Compassion
- Justice
- Respect
- Self-control
- Gratitude
- Faithfulness
- Perseverance
- Excellence

Character is not taught through lectures alone.

It is formed through repeated choices, habits, accountability, reflection, and example.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who can be trusted with influence.

__DIMENSION THREE__

__Intellectual Formation__

Knowledge is important.

Wisdom is essential.

Students are taught to:

- Think critically.
- Think creatively.
- Ask meaningful questions.
- Evaluate evidence.
- Solve problems.
- Learn independently.
- Communicate effectively.
- Pursue truth.
- Integrate knowledge across disciplines.

DGENIE develops thinkers, not merely memorizers.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who understands how to think, not merely what to think.

__DIMENSION FOUR__

__Emotional Formation__

Emotional maturity is essential for healthy leadership and relationships.

Students learn to:

- Understand their emotions.
- Manage emotional responses.
- Build resilience.
- Practice empathy.
- Resolve conflict peacefully.
- Develop healthy relationships.
- Respond wisely under pressure.
- Demonstrate self-awareness.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who possesses emotional intelligence and inner stability.

__DIMENSION FIVE__

__Social Formation__

Human beings flourish in community.

Students learn to:

- Serve others.
- Communicate respectfully.
- Collaborate effectively.
- Lead responsibly.
- Appreciate diversity.
- Build healthy relationships.
- Resolve disagreements constructively.
- Contribute positively to their communities.

Leadership is understood as service rather than status.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who strengthens every community they join.

__DIMENSION SIX__

__Vocational Formation__

Every learner is called to meaningful contribution.

Students gradually discover the connection between:

Identity

↓

Genius

↓

Purpose

↓

Calling

↓

Contribution

↓

Legacy

Education therefore prepares students not simply for employment, but for faithful stewardship of their gifts.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who lives with purpose and serves through meaningful work.

__DIMENSION SEVEN__

__Creative Formation__

Creativity reflects the image of the Creator.

Every learner is encouraged to:

- Imagine.
- Design.
- Invent.
- Build.
- Write.
- Compose.
- Perform.
- Create solutions.
- Express beauty.
- Innovate responsibly.

Creativity is viewed as a means of solving problems and serving humanity.

__Desired Outcome__

A graduate who contributes original value to the world.

__THE DGENIE FORMATION CYCLE__

Every area of formation follows the same developmental pathway.

__Inspiration__

The learner encounters truth, beauty, or an example worthy of imitation.

↓

__Instruction__

The learner receives understanding, guidance, and knowledge.

↓

__Practice__

The learner repeatedly applies what has been learned.

↓

__Reflection__

The learner examines actions, motives, and outcomes.

↓

__Correction__

Mistakes become opportunities for growth.

↓

__Habit__

Repeated practice develops consistent behaviour.

↓

__Character__

Habits shape identity.

↓

__Influence__

Character shapes families, communities, institutions, and nations.

__THE ENVIRONMENTS OF FORMATION__

Formation occurs through every aspect of school life.

These environments include:

- Classroom learning
- Family partnership
- Worship
- Storytelling
- Literature
- Music
- Drama
- Sports
- Chess
- Community service
- Leadership opportunities
- Clubs and societies
- Mentorship
- Peer relationships
- School traditions
- Daily routines
- Personal reflection
- Digital learning environments

Every environment teaches.

Therefore every environment shall be intentionally designed.

__THE ROLE OF TEACHERS__

Teachers are not merely subject specialists.

They are:

- Mentors
- Coaches
- Disciple-makers
- Character models
- Talent developers
- Encouragers
- Leaders
- Lifelong learners

The life of the teacher is considered part of the curriculum.

Students learn as much from who teachers are as from what teachers teach.

__THE ROLE OF PARENTS__

Parents are the child's first and most influential educators.

DGENIE therefore views parents as partners in formation.

The school exists to strengthen—not replace—the formative role of the family.

Parents are encouraged to model the same values, virtues, and habits promoted within the school.

__THE ROLE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE__

Artificial Intelligence supports formation without replacing human relationships.

AI may assist by:

- Personalising learning.
- Tracking growth patterns.
- Encouraging reflective practice.
- Identifying strengths and struggles.
- Supporting mentorship.
- Recommending learning experiences.
- Providing timely feedback.

AI shall never replace the wisdom of parents, teachers, mentors, or the work of the Holy Spirit in the life of the learner.

__ASSESSING FORMATION__

Formation cannot be measured by examination scores alone.

DGENIE therefore evaluates growth through multiple forms of evidence, including:

- Academic performance
- Teacher observations
- Mentor reports
- Parent feedback
- Student reflection journals
- Leadership experiences
- Service projects
- Creative portfolios
- Team participation
- Problem-solving tasks
- Behavioural growth
- Long-term habits

The primary assessment question is:

__Who is this learner becoming?__

__THE DGENIE COMMITMENT__

DGENIE commits itself to educating the whole child.

We refuse to separate knowledge from wisdom, talent from character, achievement from integrity, leadership from service, or success from purpose.

Every learner entrusted to DGENIE shall be intentionally formed to love God, serve others, pursue excellence, think wisely, lead humbly, and leave a lasting legacy.

__THE DGENIE FORMATION DECLARATION__

We believe that every child is created by God with extraordinary worth and unique purpose.

Therefore, education must extend beyond the classroom to shape the whole person.

We commit ourselves to forming learners who:

- Know God.
- Live with integrity.
- Think wisely.
- Love deeply.
- Lead humbly.
- Create boldly.
- Serve faithfully.
- Build courageously.
- Pursue excellence.
- Leave every place better than they found it.

For education has fulfilled its highest purpose only when knowledge becomes wisdom, gifts become service, and learning becomes a life that transforms the world.

__This is the DGENIE Formation Framework.__
