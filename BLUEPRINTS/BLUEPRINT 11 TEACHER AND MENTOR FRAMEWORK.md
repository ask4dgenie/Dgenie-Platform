## SECTION ONE — PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Framework governs every individual who delivers instruction or guidance within DGENIE: Teachers, bound to the Home Track and to subject-specific instruction within a single domain, never the Membership Track and never whole-child formation; Learning Coaches, providing day-to-day execution support; Mentors, holding one learner's whole child, across every domain and across their whole time at DGENIE — deep domain guidance, formation across all seven dimensions of Blueprint 03 including spiritual formation, and guidance through Signature Experience and Masterpiece work, combined in a single relationship rather than split across specialists; and Community Mentors, leading within a Local Learning Circle, a distinct governance office rather than a specialist serving one child, defined in full in Blueprint 08 Part One.

As of 2026-08-14, Mentor is deliberately one role, not five. What this Framework and Blueprint 08 previously named as five separate specialist mentors — Primary, Formation, Subject, Project, and Faith Mentor — are now one person for a given learner. A child does not accumulate a roster of specialist adults; they have one Mentor who holds their whole story, the same way Blueprint 04 Part Thirteen has always described a single "the mentor" without naming any of those five sub-types itself.

This Framework does not replace day-to-day performance management. It establishes the constitutional standards every teaching role is held to, leaving ordinary supervisory practice to operational policy.

## SECTION TWO — FOUNDATIONAL TEACHER PRINCIPLES

Every teacher is a mentor, not merely an instructor, consistent with the Vision established in Blueprint 01. Every teacher affirms the Teacher Creed, reproduced in full at Section Three. Every teacher reads the learner before planning the lesson, per Blueprint 04. Every teacher remains a lifelong learner, developing their own practice rather than treating their training as complete upon hiring. Every teacher is accountable to the mission, the learner, and the community simultaneously, never to only one of the three. Every teacher is a steward of the learner's genius, per the Stewardship of Learners principle in Blueprint 10, entrusted with something that was never theirs to own.

---

## SECTION THREE — TEACHER CREED AFFIRMATION

All teachers affirm the Teacher Creed upon hiring and annually thereafter. The affirmation is documented and stored in the teacher's personnel record. Failure to affirm the Creed is grounds for termination. The Creed is the constitutional foundation of teaching practice within DGENIE, and this Framework embeds it here in full, exactly as established in Blueprint 04, rather than merely referencing it.

__THE DGENIE TEACHER CREED__

Every DGENIE teacher and mentor affirms this creed. It is a declaration, not an instructional manual, and it governs the spirit in which every part of this Framework is practiced.

I believe every learner is capable of growth, regardless of where they currently stand on the Genius Level scale.

I believe curiosity precedes instruction, and I will ask before I tell.

I believe relationships precede influence, and no learner will be led by someone who has not first been trusted.

I believe questions often teach more than answers, and I will resist the urge to finish a learner's thought for them.

I believe mistakes are evidence of learning, and I will treat a wrong answer as data, not as failure.

I believe mastery matters more than speed, and I will never advance a learner because a calendar turned rather than because evidence was earned.

I believe character is more important than achievement, and I will not let talent excuse what character should have prevented.

I believe every learner deserves to be understood before being instructed, and I will read the learner before I plan the lesson.

I believe every lesson develops Think, Create, Lead, Serve, and Build, and I will not let a lesson become only content.

I believe every learner possesses God-given genius, and my task is to discover it, not to manufacture it.

__This is the DGENIE Teacher Creed.__

## SECTION FOUR — QUALIFICATIONS AND HIRING STANDARDS

__Teachers__ hold subject expertise, through a degree or demonstrated equivalent experience; pedagogical training or demonstrated equivalent skill; and evident alignment with DGENIE's Vision.

__Learning Coaches__ hold experience in educational support, understanding of the Genius Cycle established in Blueprint 04, and the ability to execute differentiated instruction under a teacher's or mentor's direction.

__Mentors__ hold deep domain expertise, mentorship experience, and certification by the Chief Educational Architect; training in the seven dimensions of the Formation Framework established in Blueprint 03, including spiritual maturity and theological grounding for its Spiritual Formation dimension; project management expertise for the Signature Experience or Masterpiece work they guide; safeguarding certification; and demonstrated character evidenced over time, not merely asserted at interview. This is a deliberately wide qualification bar, reflecting Section One's consolidation of what were previously five separate specialist mentor roles into this one. __Added 2026-08-15:__ this qualification bar is unchanged by Blueprint 03's parental-choice mechanism for Spiritual Formation; every Mentor is trained to deliver that Dimension's Christian-specific content and its non-doctrinal alternative equally well, and which version a given learner actually receives is set by that learner's family's enrollment choice, per Blueprint 12, not by the Mentor's own formation or preference. __Added 2026-08-15, same day:__ a family that chooses the Christian-specific version may separately request a Mentor who shares that faith background, recorded and honored per Blueprint 12 Section Five, where caseload availability allows, at first assignment and at any later rotation or renewal under this Section. This is a matching preference, not a qualification standard; a Mentor who does not share a family's specific faith background is not thereby less qualified to deliver the Christian-specific Dimension well, per the sentence above, and a family that hasn't requested the match is never assigned as though they had.

__Community Mentors__ hold local community engagement experience, leadership skill, and safeguarding certification.

Every teaching role, regardless of category, requires background screening per Blueprint 07, safeguarding training before any contact with a learner, and demonstration of the Teacher Creed's spirit during the interview process itself, not merely a promise to affirm it once hired.

__Mentor term and rotation, added 2026-08-15, incorporating the idea Duncan selected from this session's brainstorm.__ A Mentor's assignment to a specific learner runs a standard term of five years, after which the learner is assigned a new Mentor. This exists as a second, structural layer of defense against the single-point-of-failure risk this session's structural audit named directly (Findings 1.1, 5.1, and 6.1): no Mentor holds one learner's whole certification history indefinitely, no matter how excellent, which is the condition under which the audit's own "invisible by design" grade-inflation risk is most likely to take root undetected. This works alongside, not instead of, the sampled Educational Audit established in Blueprint 15 Part Two Section Seven; the two are independent checks against the same risk.

Two named exceptions temper what would otherwise be a blunt rule. First, where a learner's first Mentor assignment begins during Circle One of Blueprint 02's Discovery pathway, roughly ages two through nine, that first term runs through the end of Circle One rather than being cut off at a fixed five-year mark that could otherwise land in the middle of a developmental stage where attachment continuity matters most; standard five-year terms begin only after that first extended term ends. Second, at the end of any term, the learner's family, the learner themselves where age-appropriate, and the Mentor may jointly request one renewal, reviewed and approved by the Chief Educational Architect, informed by the Mentor Control Tower signals established in Blueprint 08 Part One, Section Three and by any relevant Blueprint 15 audit findings. No more than one renewal is available per Mentor-learner pairing, a hard ceiling of two consecutive terms, ten years, with the same Mentor before rotation becomes mandatory regardless of request, closing the loophole an unlimited renewal would otherwise leave for a single Mentor to quietly become irreplaceable again by simply always being renewed.

Once a learner reaches Legacy Induction, per Blueprint 06 Part Nine, this rotation requirement no longer applies; the assigned-Mentor structure already transforms at that point into the Mentoring Network described there, and no further mandatory rotation is layered on top of a structure that has already changed in kind. Blueprint 07 Part Two and Part Five state how the right to a trusted adult and the standards for a Mentor relationship are honored across a planned rotation, including the required handoff protocol; this Section states only that rotation happens, not how it is conducted.

__Mentor caseload ceiling, added 2026-08-15, on Duncan's direction, closing the number Blueprint 10 Part Two Section Five's unmodeled-assumption paragraph has been waiting on.__ A Mentor holds no more than twenty assigned learners at once, combined across both tracks rather than twenty per track, absent a future revision stating otherwise. This is adopted as a starting ceiling, in Duncan's own words, not asserted as a figure independently derived from caseload-cost evidence that does not yet exist; it is the number Blueprint 10's own text requires be checked against the Mentor compensation rate defined there and against the ten-percent scholarship floor before being treated as final, and that check is the immediate next step, not yet completed here. Nothing in this ceiling distinguishes Home Track time-per-learner from Membership Track time-per-learner, and Section One above and Blueprint 04 Part Five both describe genuinely different cadences for the two, a weekly check-in plus termly Portfolio Review on one side, a Mentor-run biweekly session plus quarterly Portfolio Review on the other; a future revision may split this into two track-specific numbers if modeling the caseload against the compensation rate shows a single combined ceiling doesn't hold both tracks fairly.

## SECTION FIVE — TRAINING AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

__Initial training,__ completed before any independent teaching role, covers DGENIE's philosophy and Vision; the Genius Cycle methodology established in Blueprint 04; differentiation and adaptation; safeguarding, per Blueprint 07; the role of My Genie inside the lesson; and portfolio evidence and assessment, per Blueprints 02, 04, and 15.

__Ongoing professional development__ includes annual training on curriculum developments; regular peer observation and feedback; mentorship by more experienced teachers; access to the Research Assistant and Knowledge Library named in Blueprint 08; and participation in a Community of Practice within the teacher's Local Learning Circle.

__Leadership development.__ Teachers showing leadership potential are identified for the leadership pipeline established in Blueprint 08, given opportunities to mentor newer teachers, and offered a defined pathway toward Mentor, or, in time, the Chief Educational Architect office itself, per Section Seven below.

---

## SECTION SIX — EVALUATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

__Annual performance review__ is conducted by the Chief Educational Architect or a delegate, drawing on age-appropriate, anonymous learner feedback; anonymous parent feedback; peer observation; portfolio evidence of learner growth in the teacher's domain; and the teacher's own self-reflection.

__Evaluation criteria__ include fidelity to the Genius Cycle, per Blueprint 04; learner growth, measured as Genius Level advancement in the teacher's domain; adherence to the Teacher Creed; safeguarding compliance; contribution to the community, including collaboration and resource sharing; and professional development engagement. The ability to adapt instruction to each learner's Genius Level Profile is a central and explicit evaluation criterion, not a soft or secondary consideration; a teacher who plans one lesson for a whole room without regard to each learner's actual profile has not met this Framework's standard, regardless of how strong that single lesson is in isolation.

__Remediation.__ A teacher who fails to meet the evaluation criteria receives a remediation plan combining mentorship, additional training, and a defined improvement timeline. Failure to remediate is grounds for termination.

## SECTION SEVEN — CAREER PROGRESSION AND ADVANCEMENT

__Teacher to Senior Teacher__ requires demonstrated excellence in the Genius Cycle, evidence of learner growth, positive evaluation across multiple cycles, and active mentorship of newer teachers.

__Senior Teacher to Master Teacher__ requires demonstrated excellence sustained over an extended period, contribution to curriculum development, published or shared educational resources, and leadership within the community.

__Master Teacher to Mentor__ requires deep domain expertise, active mentorship of both teachers and learners, and certification by the Chief Educational Architect.

__Mentor to Chief Educational Architect,__ where this path is pursued, follows the leadership pipeline established in Blueprint 08: demonstrated leadership and constitutional fidelity, alignment with the Vision in Blueprint 01, and inclusion in the office's own succession plan. No teacher advances to a constitutional office through this Framework alone; Blueprint 08's full succession process governs that final step.

---

## SECTION EIGHT — TEACHER AS MENTOR

Teachers are human mentors and are never replaced by AI, per Blueprint 04's principle that AI never replaces human relationships, and its explicit distinction between the teacher, the mentor, and the Genie, each holding a different, non-substitutable role.

Teachers work alongside My Genie, using the Genie's flagged patterns and portfolio evidence to personalize instruction, without ever treating a Genie observation as a verdict rather than raw evidence to be interpreted.

Teachers hold final authority on instructional judgment within their own domain; this authority is never delegated to an AI system, per Blueprint 13's constitutional purpose for artificial intelligence.

Teachers collaborate with the learner's Mentor to hold the whole child, consistent with how the teacher's subject-level view and the mentor's whole-child view fit together.

---

## SECTION NINE — SAFEGUARDING INTEGRATION

Every teacher undergoes background screening before any contact with a learner, per Blueprint 07. Every teacher completes safeguarding training initially and annually. Every teacher is a mandatory reporter, per Blueprint 07. Every teacher is subject to the Community Standards established in Blueprint 07.

A safeguarding concern involving a teacher is escalated through the crisis principles in Blueprint 07, exactly as any other safeguarding concern. The Chief Safeguarding Officer, named among the offices in Blueprint 08, may pause or remove any teacher found to pose a safeguarding risk, independent of the ordinary evaluation and remediation process in Section Six.

Safeguarding is not a stage of this Framework; it is present at hiring under Section Four, at training under Section Five, at evaluation under Section Six, and at career progression under Section Seven, since a person unsuited to work with learners is unsuited at every one of those stages, not only at the point of hire.

## SECTION TEN — RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER CONSTITUTIONAL OFFICES

The __Chief Educational Architect__ holds ultimate authority over teacher qualifications, training, and evaluation, per Blueprint 08.

The __Board of Stewards__ approves significant teacher-related policy, including compensation structure and hiring standards, per Blueprint 08.

The __Chief Safeguarding Officer__ holds independent authority to act on safeguarding concerns involving any teacher, per Section Nine above.

The __Chief Community Officer__ coordinates community-level teaching roles, specifically Community Mentors, across DGENIE's Local Learning Circles.

The __Constitutional Council__ reviews any proposed change to this Framework that touches a constitutional principle beyond ordinary amendment, per the process in Section Thirteen.

## SECTION ELEVEN — COMPENSATION AND CONDITIONS

Teachers are compensated fairly, reflecting their qualifications, experience, and performance. Compensation is reviewed annually and benchmarked against relevant markets. __Added 2026-08-15:__ a Mentor's compensation rate, twenty percent of the Membership Track subscription fee per assigned learner, and the same flat per-learner amount on the Home Track rather than a percentage of that track's higher fee, is defined in Blueprint 10 Part Two Section Five, not restated here in full. Worth naming directly: a rate expressed as a share of subscription revenue per learner, rather than a share tied to certification pace or advancement rate, is inherently compatible with the wall the next paragraph states, since nothing about it rewards certifying a learner faster or more generously.

Compensation is never tied to learner test scores or Genius Level advancement in a way that incentivizes grade inflation. Blueprint 04 is explicit that a teacher who quietly lowers the evidence standard itself, rather than adjusting the path to reach it, has stopped practicing mastery learning and started practicing grade inflation by another name; a compensation structure that rewards advancement rates directly would create exactly the incentive that principle warns against, and this Framework forbids it. __Added 2026-08-15:__ this same wall applies to the Mentor Control Tower established in Blueprint 08 Part One, Section Three: the caseload-strain signals it surfaces to a Regional Steward exist to trigger support and rebalancing, never as an input to a Mentor's compensation, evaluation, or advancement, and no Chief, Steward, or Leader may use it as one.

Teachers are entitled to professional development opportunities, including conference attendance and research access, per Section Five.

## SECTION TWELVE — MONITORING AND REVIEW

This Framework is reviewed annually by the Chief Educational Architect. The Oversight Body established in Blueprint 09 may audit teacher practices as part of its Constitutional Audit. Teacher feedback is collected regularly and used to improve this Framework over time, rather than treating it as fixed once adopted.

## SECTION THIRTEEN — AMENDMENT OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Amendments to this Framework require a proposal from the Chief Educational Architect and approval by the Board of Stewards, applying the Framework Amendments tier of the Amendment Process established in Blueprint 08.

## CONSTITUTIONAL DEFINITIONS

__Teacher.__ A person providing subject-specific instruction within DGENIE, per Section One.

__Learning Coach.__ A person providing day-to-day execution support for differentiated instruction, per Section One.

__Mentor.__ A single person holding one learner's whole-child relationship across every domain and their entire time at DGENIE — deep domain guidance, formation across all seven dimensions of Blueprint 03 including spiritual formation, and Signature Experience and Masterpiece guidance, combined rather than split across specialists — per Section One and Blueprint 04 Part Thirteen. Distinct from Community Mentor, a Local Learning Circle governance office defined in Blueprint 08 Part One rather than a per-learner relationship.

__Master Teacher.__ A teacher who has demonstrated sustained excellence, contributed to curriculum development, and shown community leadership, per Section Seven.

__Teacher Creed Affirmation.__ The documented, annual act of affirming the Teacher Creed required of every teacher and mentor, per Section Three.

__Remediation Plan.__ The mentorship, training, and timeline established for a teacher who has not met evaluation criteria, per Section Six.

__Differentiation.__ The practice of adjusting scope, pacing, and framing to a learner's Genius Level Profile without altering the evidence bar required to advance, per Blueprint 04 and Section Six above.

__Grade Inflation.__ The lowering of an evidence standard itself rather than adjusting the path to reach it, forbidden under Blueprint 04 and explicitly guarded against in this Framework's compensation structure, per Section Eleven.
