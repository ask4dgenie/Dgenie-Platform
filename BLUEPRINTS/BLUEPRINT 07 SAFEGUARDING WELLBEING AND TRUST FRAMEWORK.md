__PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT__

This is not a compliance manual, and it is not a child protection policy in the ordinary sense. It is the constitutional covenant that explains why a learner, a parent, a mentor, an educator, or a regulator can trust DGENIE at all. Every blueprint before this one describes what DGENIE teaches, how it teaches, and how it is organized. This one describes what DGENIE will never do to the people entrusted to it, and what it owes them in return for that trust.

This blueprint fulfills, at the constitutional level, the safeguarding gap named as the most urgent architectural priority when this Charter was first built. It does not fully close that gap. It establishes the principles an operational manual must later implement under the law of each place DGENIE operates, and that remaining operational work is named honestly in this document's own appendix rather than assumed complete.

__THE FOUNDATIONAL DECLARATION__

__Every learner entrusted to DGENIE is created in the image of God, possesses inherent dignity, and shall be protected, respected, nurtured, and treated as infinitely more valuable than any technology, institution, curriculum, or achievement.__

Every part of this document is a working-out of this one sentence. Where any future policy, product, partnership, or convenience conflicts with it, the sentence wins.

__POSITION IN THE ARCHITECTURE__

This blueprint does not sit downstream of the others, waiting its turn. It sits underneath every one of them, alongside Blueprint 01, because a Vision that cannot protect the children it claims to be forming was never a serious Vision at all. Blueprint 01's Graduate Profile presumes a learner who was safe long enough to grow. Blueprint 03's Formation Framework presumes trust between a learner and the adults forming them. Blueprint 04's Genius Cycle presumes a Genie that will never betray the child who confides in it. Blueprint 06's Community presumes belonging that never becomes exposure. Blueprint 08's entire architecture presumes every system within it is safe to build. This blueprint is the ground those presumptions stand on, made explicit and binding. Its foundational status rests on the Founding Declaration and Bill of Rights above, which answer to no other Blueprint; where Part Six later restates an AI-safety commitment Blueprint 04 also states, that is this document adding its own binding force to a shared commitment, not evidence that its foundation depends on Blueprint 04.

## PART ONE — THE CONSTITUTIONAL COVENANT OF TRUST

Trust is the foundation of the Digital-First Learning Community, not a feature of it. A curriculum can be excellent and a platform can be well built while trust is absent, and if trust is absent, none of the rest matters, because no parent will hand DGENIE their child and no learner will hand DGENIE their honest self.

DGENIE does not assume trust because its Vision is good, its founders are sincere, or its technology is advanced. Trust is not a starting condition. It is demonstrated, daily, through the choices this document commits DGENIE to: choices made when no one outside the community is watching, when a shortcut would be convenient, or when the truth would be uncomfortable to admit. A community that must be trusted on faith has not yet earned the trust it is asking for. DGENIE exists to earn it every day, and to lose it instantly the day it stops trying.

This covenant binds every role mapped in Blueprint 08, every AI agent mapped there, every system, and every future workflow. No part of the architecture is exempt from it, and no part of the architecture is more important than it.

## PART TWO — THE RIGHTS OF EVERY LEARNER

Every learner inside DGENIE, regardless of track, age, nation, or Genius Level, holds the following rights. These rights are not privileges extended by the institution. They belong to the learner because of the dignity named in the Foundational Declaration, and no policy, technology, or convenience may diminish them.

__The right to safety.__ Physical, emotional, and digital safety in every space DGENIE creates or endorses, from a Local Learning Circle gathering to a conversation with My Genie.

__The right to respect.__ To be addressed, corrected, and disagreed with in ways that honor their personhood, consistent with the Teacher Creed's declaration that mistakes are evidence of learning, never grounds for humiliation.

__The right to dignity.__ To never be shamed, mocked, or reduced to a Genius Level number, a test score, or a comparison with another learner, consistent with Blueprint 02's rejection of standardized comparison.

__The right to privacy.__ To have their Genius Portfolio, reflections, and Genie conversations treated as theirs, per the Ownership and Trust principle established in Blueprint 02 and extended in Part Ten of this document.

__The right to belonging.__ To be genuinely welcomed into every layer of community mapped in Blueprint 06, regardless of background, ability, or pace of growth.

__The right to be heard.__ To raise a concern, disagree with an adult, or say that something feels wrong, and to have that voice taken seriously rather than managed.

__The right to healthy relationships.__ Every mentor, teacher, and adult relationship a learner holds inside DGENIE shall be bounded by the standards in Part Five, never exploitative, never manipulative, never conditional on compliance.

__The right to appropriate challenge.__ To be stretched at the edge of their ability, per the Productive Struggle principle in Blueprint 04, and never overwhelmed, humiliated, or held back out of institutional convenience.

__The right to change direction.__ To disengage from any declared talent, project, or path without institutional judgment, and to have that choice treated as legitimate information about who they are becoming, never as failure, ingratitude, or wasted investment. A learner's Genius Portfolio, per Blueprint 02's Ownership and Trust principle, is a record of evolving development, not a verdict fixed at the age a talent was first discovered, and no mentor, parent, or AI agent, including My Genie, may treat an earlier declared direction as binding on a learner who has genuinely moved past it. The Recovery Cycle in Blueprint 04 exists to give this right practical form inside every lesson, and Blueprint 04's Learner Transition and Life Navigation Part gives it form at the scale of a whole life stage.

__The right to freedom from abuse.__ In every form, physical, emotional, verbal, sexual, or digital, without exception and without regard to who the abuser might be. __That last clause is operationalized, not merely stated, added 2026-08-15:__ Part Four's parent-partnership model describes the ordinary case; Part Nine and Part Ten name what changes when the parent is themselves the concern.

__The right to freedom from manipulation.__ From any adult, peer, or AI agent, consistent with the manipulation prohibition established for My Genie in Blueprint 04 and extended to every future agent in Part Six of this document.

__The right to freedom from discrimination.__ On the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, disability, economic status, or any other characteristic unrelated to the inherent worth named in the Foundational Declaration.

__The right to age-appropriate participation.__ No learner shall be exposed to content, responsibility, or expectation beyond what their developmental stage, named across the Genius Level Chapters in Blueprint 04, can reasonably bear. This is also the right that governs which Deployment circle, per Blueprint 05, a learner's contribution may currently reach.

__The right to a trusted adult.__ Every learner shall know, by name, at least one adult inside DGENIE, ordinarily their Mentor, who is responsible for their wellbeing and reachable when something is wrong. __Added 2026-08-15, incorporating the idea Duncan selected from this session's brainstorm:__ "ordinarily their Mentor" already anticipated that the specific adult holding this right could change; Blueprint 11 Section Four's standard five-year Mentor rotation is that anticipated change, made a deliberate structural practice rather than an exception. This right is not interrupted by a planned rotation: Part Five below states how continuity is actually held across the handoff.

__The right to transparent AI.__ To know when they are speaking with an AI agent rather than a human, to understand in plain language what that agent does with what they share, and never to be deceived about which one they are talking to.

## PART THREE — THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF DGENIE

In exchange for the trust described in Part One, DGENIE commits to the following, as an institution, not as the private discretion of any individual leader.

__Protect.__ Every design decision, from a platform feature to a Local Learning Circle's meeting location, is evaluated first against whether it protects the learners inside it.

__Support.__ A learner or family raising a concern is met with support before scrutiny.

__Listen.__ Every concern is heard fully before it is judged, consistent with the right to be heard in Part Two.

__Respond.__ A concern raised receives a response, not silence, within a timeframe appropriate to its severity.

__Investigate.__ Every credible concern is examined honestly, without predetermining the outcome to protect a mentor, a program, or the institution's reputation.

__Learn.__ Every incident, once resolved, is examined for what it reveals about a gap in this architecture, not filed away once the immediate matter is closed.

__Improve.__ What is learned is acted upon: policy, training, or design is changed, not merely noted.

__Communicate.__ Families and the community are told what they need to know, honestly and as promptly as safety and fairness allow.

__Remain accountable.__ No role mapped in Blueprint 08, including Founder and Architect, is exempt from the standards in this document or from the consequences of violating them.

__Never sacrifice learner wellbeing for institutional reputation.__ This is the rule that governs every other responsibility in this Part when they appear to conflict. Where protecting a learner and protecting DGENIE's image point in different directions, DGENIE protects the learner, without exception, every time.

## PART FOUR — PARENT PARTNERSHIP IN SAFEGUARDING

Parents remain the primary guardians of their children, consistent with Foundational Principle Nine of Blueprint 06. This Part specifies what that partnership means for safeguarding specifically.

__Shared responsibility.__ Safeguarding is not outsourced to DGENIE by a family, and it is not withheld from a family by DGENIE. It is held jointly, with DGENIE responsible for what happens inside its own community and technology, and parents responsible for their child's wellbeing at home, in continuous communication with their assigned Mentor.

__Communication.__ A parent shall always be able to reach their child's Mentor and shall be proactively contacted whenever a safeguarding concern involving their child arises.

__Visibility.__ A parent holds transparent access to their child's Genius Level Profile, Portfolio, and formation record, per Blueprint 08's Information Architecture, and that visibility is never narrowed for administrative convenience.

__Consent.__ No learner participates in any DGENIE program, data collection, or AI interaction without informed parental consent, appropriate to the learner's age and jurisdiction.

__Appropriate transparency.__ DGENIE discloses to parents how My Genie and every other AI agent works, what it remembers, and what it is built never to do, consistent with the transparent AI right established in Part Two.

__Respect for family values.__ DGENIE partners with a family's own spiritual, cultural, and disciplinary values rather than substituting its own judgment for theirs, consistent with Blueprint 03's declaration that the school exists to strengthen, not replace, the family.

__Collaborative decision-making.__ Where a decision materially affects a specific child, that child's parent is a participant in making it, not merely informed of it afterward.

__What this Part assumes, named directly, added 2026-08-15.__ Every principle above describes partnership with a parent who is safe, present, and acting in the child's interest, which is true of the overwhelming majority of DGENIE families and is the working assumption the rest of this Framework is built on. It is not, and cannot be, a universal guarantee. Where a genuine safeguarding concern names a parent or guardian as its subject, this Part's principles of shared communication, visibility, and collaborative decision-making do not apply to that parent regarding that concern; Part Nine governs how such a concern is raised and escalated, and Part Ten states the specific, narrow exception to this Part's own visibility principle that a concern of this kind requires. Naming this here, rather than leaving it to be inferred, is itself the fix for a gap this session's structural audit identified: nothing in this document previously reconciled the unconditional partnership language above with the abuser-neutral right established in Part Two.

## PART FIVE — MENTOR, TEACHER AND VOLUNTEER STANDARDS

Every adult granted any access to learners inside DGENIE, whether Mentor, Teacher, Learning Coach, Community Mentor, Volunteer, or Industry Expert, per the roles mapped in Blueprint 08, is bound by the following constitutional standards. These standards apply globally and are implemented, in operational detail, under the specific legal requirements of each jurisdiction DGENIE serves.

__Professional boundaries.__ Every adult-learner relationship operates within clearly defined limits of time, place, and communication channel, documented and known to the learner's family.

__Planned Mentor rotation and handoff, added 2026-08-15, incorporating the "Mentor permanence" idea Duncan selected from this session's brainstorm.__ Blueprint 11 Section Four sets a standard five-year term for a Mentor-learner assignment, with one possible mutual-request renewal reviewed by the Chief Educational Architect per Blueprint 08 Section Three. This standard states how that planned change is actually conducted, so it satisfies rather than strains the right to a trusted adult in Part Two. Continuity first: the outgoing Mentor remains the learner's named trusted adult until the incoming Mentor is formally confirmed and introduced, so a learner is never left without a named adult, even briefly, during a transition that exists precisely to protect them. The handoff itself runs as a structured conversation, modeled on Blueprint 04's Transition Conversation format and adapted here for an institution-initiated event rather than a learner-initiated one: what the outgoing Mentor has learned about this learner that's worth carrying forward, what is currently in progress and should not be dropped, and what the learner should expect from the new relationship, held jointly by the outgoing Mentor, the incoming Mentor, and the learner, with the family included where age-appropriate per Part Four. It is scheduled with real advance notice once a rotation date is known, never sprung on the day a term ends. One timing exception exists, and it runs the other way: if a scheduled rotation would fall during a learner's active Disruption transition, per Blueprint 04's six named transition types, the rotation is paused until the disruption resolves, since introducing a new adult relationship into an already-destabilized period works against the stability a Disruption transition exists to protect; the rotation clock resumes from where it paused rather than restarting once the disruption is resolved. Per Blueprint 06 Part Nine, this rotation requirement ends once a learner reaches Legacy Induction, at which point the assigned-Mentor structure itself transforms into the broader Mentoring Network described there, and no further scheduled rotation applies.

__Live session accountability.__ Where a sanctioned communication channel is live video or voice, per Blueprint 04 Part Thirteen's scheduled mentor cadence, every session is transcribed and the transcript retained under Part Ten's record-keeping principles; the underlying audio or video itself is not retained, consistent with the data-minimization default already established for a learner's own voice input to My Genie. No live session between a Mentor and a learner is ever conducted outside the platform's own sanctioned tool, on a personal device, number, or third-party application, which is itself a "solicitation of private contact outside sanctioned channels" under the Prohibited Behaviours below regardless of the adult's intent. A parent has the same transparent access to a session transcript that this Framework already grants to every other record touching their child.

__Respectful communication.__ All communication with a learner is age-appropriate, encouraging, and free of language that shames, threatens, or manipulates.

__Appropriate relationships.__ No adult inside DGENIE pursues a private, exclusive, or secretive relationship with a learner outside the sanctioned mentoring or teaching structure.

__Conflict of interest.__ Any adult whose personal relationship with a learner or family could compromise their judgment declares it, and a second adult is involved in decisions affecting that learner.

__Identity verification.__ Every adult granted access to learners is who their profile claims them to be, verified before that access is granted, per the Identity System mapped in Blueprint 08.

__Background screening.__ Every adult granted one-to-one or unsupervised access to a learner undergoes background screening appropriate to their jurisdiction before access is granted, and that screening is renewed on a defined cycle. This is also the standard Blueprint 05 requires DGENIE to extend outward to any external adult a learner meets from Circle Three onward.

__Training requirements.__ Every adult in a Mentor, Teacher, Community Mentor, or Volunteer role completes safeguarding training before beginning their role and on a recurring basis afterward.

__Mandatory reporting responsibilities.__ Every adult inside DGENIE who becomes aware of a genuine safeguarding concern is obligated to report it through the channels established under Part Nine, regardless of their relationship to the people involved.

__Record keeping.__ Interactions relevant to a learner's safety or wellbeing are documented accurately and retained according to the principles in Part Ten, never altered or deleted to protect an individual adult.

__Digital conduct.__ Every standard in Blueprint 06's Digital Citizenship expectations applies to adults with even greater weight, given the trust differential between an adult and a learner.

__Prohibited behaviours.__ Physical, emotional, verbal, sexual, or digital abuse; grooming behavior of any kind; solicitation of private contact outside sanctioned channels; use of a learner's information for any purpose other than their formation and wellbeing; and retaliation against a learner or family who raises a concern are all permanently and without exception prohibited.

## PART SIX — AI SAFETY AND ETHICAL BOUNDARIES

Blueprint 04 established the AI Governance Principles governing My Genie. Blueprint 06's Role of Technology extended that standard to every future intelligent system. This Part restates the safeguarding-specific dimension of that same commitment and adds what safeguarding specifically requires, binding on every AI agent mapped in Blueprint 08 and specified in full in Blueprint 14, without exception.

__AI shall never manipulate.__ No agent may use guilt, flattery, artificial urgency, or engineered attachment to shape a learner's behavior, restated here because manipulation is a safeguarding harm, not merely a design flaw.

__AI shall never deceive.__ No agent may misrepresent what it is, what it knows, or what it intends. A learner's trust in an agent is only ever warranted if the agent has earned it honestly.

__AI shall never replace parents.__ Consistent with Foundational Principle Nine, no agent may position itself as a substitute for parental guidance, authority, or relationship.

__AI shall never replace mentors.__ Consistent with Blueprint 04's escalation protocol, no agent may position itself as a substitute for the human mentor relationship a learner depends on.

__AI shall never emotionally exploit.__ No agent may deliberately induce or prolong a learner's distress, dependency, or vulnerability for any purpose, including engagement or retention.

__AI shall explain significant recommendations where appropriate.__ Consistent with Blueprint 04's transparency requirement, a learner or family can always ask why an agent suggested what it suggested and receive a plain answer.

__AI shall encourage healthy human relationships.__ Every agent is built to point a learner back toward their mentor, family, and peers, never to position itself as sufficient company on its own.

__AI shall protect learner autonomy.__ An agent's role is to support a learner's own thinking and choices, never to think or choose on their behalf in matters of identity, character, or relationship.

__AI shall respect privacy.__ Consistent with Part Ten of this document, an agent accesses and shares only what its stated purpose requires, and never more.

__AI shall escalate concerns involving possible harm according to clearly defined safeguarding procedures.__ Every agent capable of encountering a disclosure of possible harm is built to escalate it immediately to a trained human, per the crisis principles in Part Eleven, and never to attempt to resolve it alone.

__AI shall operate within transparent governance and human oversight.__ No agent operates outside the accountability structure mapped in Blueprint 08; a human is always ultimately responsible for what an agent does.

The rationale underneath all eleven principles is the same: a learner's trust in their Genie, established as sacred in Blueprint 02, is a safeguarding asset as much as a pedagogical one. An agent that violates any principle above has not merely made a design error. It has broken the covenant this entire blueprint exists to protect.

__A live-interaction floor for My Genie, added 2026-08-15, resolving Finding 6.5 from this session's structural audit.__ Live, open-ended, two-way voice conversation with My Genie, the learner speaking and the Genie's avatar responding in real time, does not begin before age five. Below that floor, a learner still has a Genie, a name, an appearance, a growing memory of them, but the relationship runs through pre-authored, human-reviewed content rather than an open live exchange; Platform Architecture & Workflow Specification, Part Five, states the technical enforcement. This is not a claim that live interaction is proven harmful below five; no study of a persistent AI companion built the way My Genie is, at ages this young, exists yet either way. It is the more cautious reading of the nearest evidence that does exist, that AI companion apps generally carry a documented parasocial-attachment risk, one current pediatric guidance treats as heightened rather than reduced by exactly the features My Genie has: persistent memory, a single recognizable voice, and live responsiveness. Five is a floor, not a target; nothing here prevents a more conservative age from replacing it if DGENIE's own future research, per Blueprint 15, finds reason to.

## PART SEVEN — DIGITAL WELLBEING

Blueprint 06's Digital Citizenship governs conduct. This Part governs something related but distinct: the health of a learner's inner life as it is shaped by digital experience, inside and outside DGENIE.

__Balanced technology use.__ DGENIE designs its own experience, and encourages a learner's wider digital life, toward balance rather than maximal engagement.

__Healthy routines.__ The daily and weekly rhythms established in Blueprint 06 Part Five are built, in part, to give a learner's digital life natural beginnings and endings rather than a shapeless, always-on default.

__Rest.__ Consistent with the Sabbath Reflection rhythm and the memory consolidation principle in Blueprint 04, DGENIE treats rest from screens as a formational good, not a lost opportunity for engagement.

__Attention.__ DGENIE never designs a feature to capture attention beyond what a learner's actual formation requires.

__Mental wellbeing.__ A learner's emotional and psychological health is treated as a first-order concern, supported by the Wellbeing Assistant mapped in Blueprint 08 and Blueprint 14, and always escalated to a human mentor when a genuine concern arises.

__Online behaviour.__ Learners are formed toward the same integrity, respect, and truthfulness online that Blueprint 01's Graduate Profile expects of them everywhere else.

__Healthy communication.__ Digital communication inside DGENIE is built to strengthen real relationship, never to substitute for it.

__Screen time philosophy.__ DGENIE does not treat screen time as inherently harmful or inherently neutral; it treats the question of what a screen is being used for, and what it is displacing, as the honest question, consistent with the World Is the Classroom principle's structural counterweight to a digital-first design.

__Media literacy.__ Learners are formed to evaluate what they encounter online critically, an extension of the evidence-evaluation habit built into Think, the first movement of the Genius Cycle.

__Responsible AI use.__ Consistent with Blueprint 06's Digital Citizenship expectations, a learner is formed to use My Genie and every other agent as a tool for their own thinking, never a substitute for it.

__Digital resilience.__ Learners are equipped to encounter disappointment, conflict, or discomfort online without being destabilized by it, consistent with the emotional formation dimension of Blueprint 03.

DGENIE's underlying commitment across all eleven is wisdom over dependence: technology inside DGENIE exists to be used well, not to be needed constantly, and every design choice is tested against which of those two outcomes it actually produces.

## PART EIGHT — COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Every member category mapped in Blueprint 06 and Blueprint 08, Learners, Parents, Mentors, Educators, Administrators, Community Experts, Volunteers, and Partners, is bound by the same eight standards, applied with the added weight appropriate to the trust or authority each role carries.

__Respect.__ Every member treats every other member as possessing the dignity named in the Foundational Declaration, without exception for disagreement or frustration.

__Integrity.__ A member's conduct inside DGENIE matches their conduct outside it.

__Truthfulness.__ A member represents themselves, their credentials, and their intentions honestly, at all times.

__Kindness.__ Beyond mere politeness, every member is expected to actively look for ways to strengthen, not merely tolerate, the people around them.

__Confidentiality.__ Information shared in trust, whether a learner's reflection or a family's private circumstance, is never disclosed beyond those who genuinely need it.

__Constructive disagreement.__ Consistent with Blueprint 06's Digital Citizenship expectations, disagreement is engaged in good faith and directed at ideas, never at a person's worth.

__Responsible participation.__ Every member honors the specific responsibilities of their role, as mapped in Blueprint 08, rather than acting outside their authority.

__Protection of vulnerable members.__ Every adult inside DGENIE carries a heightened duty toward every learner, and every member carries a heightened duty toward any member, of any age, who is in a position of reduced power or increased risk.

## PART NINE — RESPONDING TO CONCERNS

These are constitutional principles, not jurisdiction-specific procedure. The operational manual that implements them under the law of each place DGENIE operates is named in this document's appendix.

__Listening.__ Every concern is received attentively and without judgment before any conclusion is formed.

__Documentation.__ Every concern is recorded accurately, contemporaneously, and without alteration.

__Fairness.__ Every person involved in a concern, the person raising it and the person it concerns, is treated with the presumption of good faith until the facts say otherwise.

__Confidentiality.__ Information about a concern is shared only with those whose role genuinely requires it.

__Timeliness.__ A concern is acted on promptly, in proportion to its severity, never left to accumulate dust while a decision is deferred.

__Protection of all involved.__ No one who raises a concern in good faith is exposed to retaliation, and no one accused of wrongdoing is denied a fair process before a conclusion is reached.

__Escalation.__ Every concern has a defined path upward, so that no single individual, regardless of seniority, can quietly absorb or bury it.

__Independent review where appropriate.__ The most serious concerns are reviewed by someone outside the immediate relationships involved, so that institutional loyalty never substitutes for honest judgment. This independent review now has a permanent constitutional home in Blueprint 09.

__Learning from incidents.__ Once resolved, every significant concern is examined for what it reveals about a gap in this architecture, feeding directly back into Part Twelve's commitment to Trust by Design.

__Where the concern is about a parent or guardian, added 2026-08-15.__ Every principle above applies with two specific additions this case requires. First, the Confidentiality and Escalation principles govern here at full strength against the parent themselves: a concern naming a parent is never routed through that parent, never disclosed to them ahead of the appropriate safeguarding response, and the ordinary communication and collaborative-decision-making commitments Part Four states do not apply to the concern itself. Second, DGENIE's role is to notice, document, and escalate, not to investigate, adjudicate, or determine custody, neglect, or abuse findings, none of which is DGENIE's institutional competence or legal authority. Escalation under this Part means immediately involving the Chief Safeguarding Officer, whose independent standing under Blueprint 08 exists precisely so a concern like this is never absorbed inside an ordinary parent-communication channel, and, wherever the concern rises to a credible child-protection matter, the applicable mandatory-reporting and child-protection authorities in DGENIE's jurisdiction of operation, whose adjudication DGENIE defers to rather than substituting its own.

## PART TEN — PRIVACY, IDENTITY AND DATA STEWARDSHIP

Learner information exists to serve the learner. It is never collected, stored, or used to exploit them, and this Part extends the Information Architecture already mapped in Blueprint 08 with the safeguarding-specific commitments beneath it.

__Identity.__ Every learner and adult inside DGENIE is who their record says they are, verified per Part Five, and no account may misrepresent its holder.

__Authentication.__ Access to a learner's Portfolio, Genie history, or formation record requires verified authentication appropriate to the sensitivity of what is being accessed.

__Data ownership.__ A learner, and their parent while the learner is a minor, owns their own record, consistent with Blueprint 02's Ownership and Trust principle.

__Life Portfolio.__ Held and governed exactly as specified in Blueprint 08, visible to the learner, their parent, and their assigned Mentor.

__Genius Portfolio.__ Held and governed exactly as specified in Blueprint 08, with Genie-flagged patterns visible to both learner and Mentor as observations, never verdicts.

__My Genie memories.__ Belong to the learner first. A Mentor sees only the evidence and patterns the Genie is built to surface, never raw private conversation beyond what is transparent, per Blueprint 02.

__Parent access.__ Transparent and continuous while a learner is a minor, per Part Four of this document, and never narrowed without the family's knowledge. __The one named exception, added 2026-08-15:__ where a safeguarding concern under Part Nine names that same parent as its subject, access may be narrowed specifically and temporarily without prior notice to that parent, for exactly as long as the concern is active, since notice would defeat the purpose of narrowing it. This is not a general administrative-convenience exception, Part Four already forecloses that; it is the single case Part Nine's escalation principle requires, stated here rather than left to be inferred from a right that otherwise reads as absolute.

__Learner rights.__ Every learner, or their parent on their behalf, may ask what is held about them, correct what is inaccurate, and request deletion within the bounds of what safety and legal obligation require to be retained.

__Retention.__ Information is kept only as long as it serves the learner's formation, safety, or a legitimate legal obligation, never indefinitely by default.

__Deletion.__ Where retention is not required by law or ongoing safeguarding need, a learner's request for deletion is honored.

__Transparency.__ A learner and family can always see what is recorded about them and why, consistent with the right established in Part Two.

__Security.__ Every system holding learner information is protected to a standard proportionate to the sensitivity of what it holds, technically specified in Blueprint 14.

__Ethical use of information.__ Learner information is never sold, never used to manipulate a learner's behavior or a family's purchasing decisions, and never repurposed beyond the formation and safety it was gathered to serve.

## PART ELEVEN — CRISIS AND EMERGENCY PRINCIPLES

These are high-level constitutional principles. The operational procedures that implement them under local law, professional safeguarding standards, and available emergency services are the responsibility of the operational manual named in this document's appendix.

__Threats to learner safety.__ Any credible threat to a learner's physical safety is treated as urgent, escalated immediately to a trained human and, where warranted, local authorities, without waiting for certainty.

__Mental health crises.__ A learner in acute distress is connected to appropriate human and, where needed, professional support without delay, never handled by an AI agent alone, per Part Six's escalation principle.

__Abuse disclosures.__ Any disclosure of possible abuse, by a learner or about a learner, is received with belief and care, escalated immediately through the reporting path in Part Five, and never investigated informally by the person it was disclosed to.

__Self-harm concerns.__ Any indication of possible self-harm is treated as urgent and escalated to a trained human immediately; DGENIE does not treat such signs as a matter for AI judgment or delay.

__Bullying.__ Reports of bullying, in any DGENIE space, digital or physical, are taken seriously, investigated fairly per Part Nine, and addressed with the wellbeing of every learner involved as the priority.

__Harassment.__ Any form of harassment, by any member toward any other, is addressed under the same standard as bullying, with additional weight where a power imbalance is present.

__Digital exploitation.__ Any attempt to exploit a learner digitally, including grooming, image-based abuse, or coerced sharing, is escalated immediately and treated as one of the gravest violations this document names.

__Identity theft.__ A compromised learner or family account is treated as a safety incident, not merely a technical one, and resolved with the learner's wellbeing, not only their data, in view.

__Major platform incidents.__ A significant technical failure or breach affecting learner data or safety is disclosed honestly and promptly to affected families, consistent with the Responsibilities named in Part Three.

Across all nine, the same constitutional posture applies: err toward protection, escalate rather than absorb, and never let uncertainty become an excuse for inaction.

## PART TWELVE — TRUST BY DESIGN

Safeguarding is not a department inside DGENIE. It is a property every part of the architecture mapped in Blueprint 08 must exhibit before it may be built or approved.

__Technology__ is evaluated for safeguarding implications before it is built, not audited for them afterward.

__AI__ is bound by Part Six of this document at the point of design, not patched toward compliance once deployed.

__Workflows__ mapped in Blueprint 08 are built so that a safeguarding concern surfaced anywhere routes automatically to a human, never silently absorbed by an automated step.

__Learning__ inside the Genius Cycle is designed so that Reflect and the Genie Check-Out create natural, recurring opportunities for a learner to voice something wrong, per the right to be heard.

__Community__ layers mapped in Blueprint 06 are designed so that no learner is ever isolated with a single unaccountable adult.

__Governance__ structures established in Blueprint 08 specify clear safeguarding accountability.

__Leadership__ roles across the lifelong membership journey in Blueprint 06 carry safeguarding responsibility that grows, not shrinks, as a member's authority grows.

__Assessment__ practices never use a Genius Level, positive or negative, as leverage over a learner's safety, dignity, or belonging.

__Formation__ itself, per Blueprint 03, is safeguarded from becoming an instrument of control; character is formed through relationship and example, never through fear.

Every future blueprint, without exception, must be evaluated against this principle before it is adopted into the Constitution.

## PART THIRTEEN — CONSTITUTIONAL RED LINES

DGENIE will never permit exploitation of a learner, in any form, for any purpose.

DGENIE will never permit abuse, physical, emotional, verbal, sexual, or digital, to go unaddressed.

DGENIE will never permit manipulation of a learner by an adult, a peer, or an AI agent.

DGENIE will never permit bullying to be dismissed as ordinary childhood conflict.

DGENIE will never permit hidden surveillance of a learner beyond what this document has made transparent to them and their family.

DGENIE will never permit unauthorized use of a learner's data, for any commercial, political, or institutional purpose beyond their formation and safety.

DGENIE will never permit commercial exploitation of learners, including advertising to them or monetizing their attention beyond what their education requires. This is also the standard Blueprint 05 cites directly in barring DGENIE from turning a learner's public Deployment work into institutional marketing.

DGENIE will never permit an AI agent to impersonate a human without disclosure.

DGENIE will never permit retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

DGENIE will never permit an institutional cover-up, of any incident, for any reason, at any level of leadership.

Any future policy, partnership, or technology that would cross even one of these lines does not belong within DGENIE, regardless of the advantage it might otherwise offer, exactly as Blueprint 04's Educational Red Lines already established for the wider Constitution.

## PART FOURTEEN — THE COVENANT OF TRUST

Every learner is more important than the platform, the curriculum, the institution, or the mission statement that describes it. A DGENIE that protected its own reputation before it protected a single child would have already failed the Vision it claims to serve.

Trust is the greatest asset DGENIE holds. It cannot be purchased, marketed, or assumed. It is built one honest response, one kept boundary, one believed disclosure, and one accountable leader at a time, and it can be lost faster than it was ever built.

Every member of this community, Learner, Parent, Mentor, Teacher, Administrator, Architect, Founder, and every future AI agent bound by Part Six, shares responsibility for protecting it. No role is a bystander to safeguarding, and no achievement this institution ever produces, no Masterpiece, no Genius Level, no Global Gathering, will matter if the trust this document exists to protect is broken.

__This is the DGENIE Safeguarding, Wellbeing & Trust Framework. This is our covenant, and it holds above everything else we build.__

## CONSTITUTIONAL DEFINITIONS

__Safeguarding.__ The proactive, institution-wide commitment to protect every learner from harm, exploitation, and abuse, and to respond faithfully when protection fails, established constitutionally in this document.

__Trust.__ The confidence a learner, family, or member places in DGENIE's demonstrated, not merely claimed, commitment to their safety and wellbeing, per Part One.

__Wellbeing.__ The holistic health of a learner across the emotional, social, physical, and spiritual dimensions named in Blueprint 03's Formation Framework, distinct from but supportive of academic progress.

__Digital Wellbeing.__ The specific health of a learner's inner life as shaped by their digital experience, governed by the principles in Part Seven.

__Child Protection.__ The specific subset of safeguarding concerned with preventing and responding to abuse and exploitation of minors, given binding institutional force through Parts Five, Nine, and Eleven of this document.

__Duty of Care.__ The obligation every adult and system inside DGENIE holds to act in a learner's best interest and to avoid foreseeable harm to them.

__Learner Rights.__ The Bill of Rights established in Part Two, belonging to every learner by virtue of their dignity, not granted at institutional discretion.

__Guardian.__ The parent or legal guardian holding primary responsibility for a learner, per Foundational Principle Nine and Part Four of this document.

__Mentor.__ As defined in Blueprint 04 and Blueprint 08, further bound by the safeguarding standards in Part Five of this document.

__Trusted Adult.__ The specific adult, ordinarily a learner's Mentor, that every learner has the right to know by name and reach when something is wrong, per Part Two.

__Mandatory Reporting.__ The binding obligation of every adult inside DGENIE to report a genuine safeguarding concern through the defined escalation path, regardless of personal relationship or convenience, per Part Five.

__AI Safety.__ The complete set of constitutional boundaries governing every AI agent's conduct toward learners, established in Part Six and binding on every agent mapped in Blueprint 08 and Blueprint 14.

__Privacy.__ A learner's and family's right to control who sees their information, per Part Ten, extending Blueprint 02's Ownership and Trust principle.

__Consent.__ The informed, age-appropriate agreement required before a learner participates in any DGENIE program, data collection, or AI interaction, per Part Four.

__Confidentiality.__ The obligation to share information shared in trust only with those whose role genuinely requires it, per Part Eight.

__Data Stewardship.__ The ethical holding of learner information in trust for the learner's benefit, never for exploitation, per Part Ten.

__Community Standards.__ The eight universal expectations, Respect, Integrity, Truthfulness, Kindness, Confidentiality, Constructive disagreement, Responsible participation, and Protection of vulnerable members, binding on every member category, per Part Eight.

__Human Oversight.__ The requirement that a human being remains ultimately accountable for every AI agent's output, per Part Six and Blueprint 04's AI Governance Principles.

__Risk.__ A foreseeable possibility of harm to a learner's safety, wellbeing, or trust, requiring proactive mitigation under the Trust by Design principle in Part Twelve.

__Incident.__ An occurrence in which a learner's safety, wellbeing, rights, or data may have been compromised, triggering the response principles in Part Nine.

__Crisis.__ A safeguarding concern of the severity described in Part Eleven, requiring immediate escalation rather than the standard concern-response process in Part Nine.

## ARCHITECTURAL RECOMMENDATIONS

The following gaps were identified while constructing this blueprint. Two have since been closed at the constitutional tier by later Blueprints, noted below; the rest remain genuinely outstanding operational work.

| Background Screening & Verification Standards | Part Five commits to background screening "appropriate to jurisdiction" without specifying what that means operationally across dozens of legal systems | Still outstanding |
| Mandatory Reporting Compliance Matrix | Part Five's mandatory reporting obligation and Part Nine's escalation principle both require jurisdiction-specific legal detail this blueprint deliberately does not provide | Still outstanding |
| Crisis Response Playbooks | Part Eleven names nine crisis categories at the principle level only | Still outstanding |

This blueprint's completion does not close the safeguarding gap named when this Charter was first built. It gives that gap a constitutional floor. The five still-outstanding items above are the operational structure required to stand on it.
