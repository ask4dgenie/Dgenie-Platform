__A NOTE ON THIS MERGER__

This Blueprint joins two documents that answer a related but distinct question, both concerning how DGENIE knows what it knows about a learner or about itself. The former Blueprint Twenty-Four, Educational Research & Knowledge Stewardship Framework, is a __Framework Blueprint__: it governs how DGENIE tests whether its own institutional hypotheses, that the Genius Cycle works, that early discovery helps, that contribution builds motivation, actually hold up at the scale of the whole community over years. The Evidence Triangulation & Assessment Synthesis Protocol is a __Technical Specification__: it governs the narrower, individual-learner question of how a mentor combines several, sometimes disagreeing, pieces of evidence about one learner into a single honest judgment. One operates at the level of the institution; the other operates at the level of a single Genius Level certification. Because they sit at different tiers of the Constitutional Discipline Rule established in Blueprint 08, and because both ultimately answer to the same Intelligence Governance principles established in Blueprint 13 Part Nine, they are reproduced here as two full Parts, each retaining its own original internal Section numbering, rather than interleaved into a single renumbered sequence. Amending Part One requires Framework Blueprint authority, per the Constitutional Amendment Process; amending Part Two requires only the Technical Changes authority already vested in the Assessment Architect, per Part Two's own Section Eleven.

Together, these two Parts close the last two items named directly, by title, in Blueprint 13's Architectural Recommendations appendix, and the lowest-priority item named in the 2026-07-24 "Platform Correction and Gaps" review's Incorporation Plan.

## SECTION TWO — PURPOSE & SCOPE

DGENIE is built on hypotheses: that the Genius Cycle produces better formation than a conventional lesson structure, that discovering a learner's genius early improves their trajectory, that a digital-first community can form character as reliably as a physical one, that contribution builds motivation more durably than reward does. Blueprint 04 already states, in detail, why the architecture is expected to work, grounded in established learning science. That is design reasoning, offered honestly as reasoning rather than as proof.

This Framework governs how DGENIE finds out whether its own hypotheses are actually holding up in practice, once real learners are living inside the architecture rather than the architecture existing only on paper. It applies to every question of the form "is this actually working," asked about any part of the Charter, and it governs how the answer, once found, is allowed to change anything.

---

## SECTION THREE — FOUNDATIONAL RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

DGENIE studies itself. A hypothesis that motivated a blueprint's design is not assumed correct forever because it was reasoned carefully at the time; it is tested against what actually happens to real learners, continuously, for as long as DGENIE exists.

Research serves learners; it does not use them. Every principle in Blueprint 13 Part Nine applies here without exception, most centrally that no source of intelligence, this Framework's research activity included, may be used in a way that violates the Constitutional Red Lines in Blueprint 07 Part Thirteen, and that privacy, per Blueprint 07 Part Ten, bounds every research activity this Framework names.

An inconvenient finding is not a threat to be managed. Consistent with the Truthfulness principle already established in Blueprint 13 Part Nine, a research finding that DGENIE's own architecture is not producing the outcome it was designed for is reported as honestly as a finding that confirms it.

__The DGENIE Research Standards.__ Every research document produced under this Framework, and every companion Research Summary or Hybrid Framework this Charter cites, sorts its own findings by one fixed hierarchy of evidentiary weight: established evidence, then influential theories, then emerging or actively contested research, then expert opinion, with any framework that is popular in practice but weakly supported by evidence stated as such directly rather than softened or omitted. This is not a new requirement; it is the standard already in continuous use, by name, across every Research Summary and Hybrid Framework this Charter has produced since 2026-08-12. It is defined here, once, so that "per DGENIE's Research Standards" cites an actual definition rather than an assumed one.

## SECTION FOUR — WHAT DGENIE RESEARCHES

This Framework names seven standing research questions, each testing a hypothesis load-bearing to the Charter. This list is illustrative of the standard, not exhaustive; the Chief Educational Architect, per Section Seven, may add a question following the same standard.

__Is the Genius Cycle implemented with fidelity, and, where it is, what do outcomes look like?__ Two distinct questions, kept distinct on purpose. The first, implementation fidelity, asks whether Mentors and Genies are actually running the seven-movement lesson structure established in Blueprint 04 as designed; DGENIE's own session data and Genie Check-In and Check-Out records answer this directly, since it is a question about compliance with a known design, not about whether the design itself is sound. The second, whether faithful implementation produces the formation and retention outcomes the underlying learning-science reasoning predicts, is the harder claim. This Framework does not treat DGENIE's own longitudinal Genius Level advancement and Genius Portfolio evidence as sufficient proof of it alone, since that evidence is produced by the very Mentors and rubric the hypothesis is about and cannot by itself distinguish "the Cycle works" from "the people certifying it believe it works." Section Five's external validation requirement exists to give this second question a check the institution's own records cannot provide.

__Does personalized, mastery-based progression improve outcomes?__ Whether the evidence-gated, uncapped-time model established in Blueprint 04 produces better long-term competency than time-based, cohort-paced instruction, where comparison data is honestly available.

__Does early genius discovery genuinely aid a learner's development?__ Whether the discovery process established in Blueprint 02, including any exercise inviting a learner to imagine a future version of themselves, correlates with stronger, more durable talent development than a comparable learner population without it, and whether the process shows any sign of the identity-lock-in risk that Blueprint 07's right to change direction and Blueprint 04's Recovery Cycle already exist to guard against.

__Does the Digital-First Community model form character as reliably as it claims?__ Whether the Local Learning Circle and layered belonging structures established in Blueprint 06 produce the character and community outcomes the model assumes, tested against the Formation Framework's own stated outcomes in Blueprint 03.

__Does the Contribution Principle improve motivation?__ Whether the Contribution requirement established in Blueprint 05 measurably strengthens a learner's engagement and sense of purpose, or functions merely as an added task.

__Does the DGENIE model produce better long-term outcomes overall?__ The composite question, asked at the scale of a full cohort over years, comparing DGENIE learners' trajectories, per the Graduate Profile established in Blueprint 01, against reasonable external benchmarks where they honestly exist.

__Does the Membership Track's fortnightly cadence sustain retention as well as weekly contact does?__ Added 2026-08-14. Blueprint 04 Part Five sets the Membership Track's dose at once every two weeks for caseload and pricing reasons, not because spacing research established fourteen days as an optimum; the Genius Cycle's existing retrieval and spacing mechanics make good use of whatever interval is chosen, which is a different claim from the interval itself being evidence-optimal. This question tests whether it actually is, holding everything else constant, rather than leaving the assumption unexamined inside a paragraph that credits it retroactively.

## SECTION FIVE — RESEARCH GOVERNANCE AND ETHICAL BOUNDARIES

Every research activity under this Framework operates inside the ten Intelligence Governance principles established in Blueprint 13 Part Nine, without restatement or exception. Three of those principles carry particular weight for research specifically and are named here for emphasis, not redefinition.

__Privacy.__ Research draws on Collective Intelligence, per Blueprint 13 Part Seven: de-identified, aggregated patterns only, never an individual learner's record without their family's explicit consent, consistent with the Analytics Agents' existing boundary that no individual learner decision is ever made by aggregate analytics alone.

__Bias mitigation.__ A research finding is tested for whether it holds equally across every group protected under Blueprint 07's freedom-from-discrimination right before it is treated as a general finding about DGENIE's model, using the same bias-testing discipline Blueprint 14 Part Two already applies to the Analytics Agents.

__No research-based exception to a Constitutional Red Line.__ A promising research finding is never grounds to cross a boundary set in Blueprint 07 Part Thirteen. Research operates inside the Charter's limits; it does not earn the authority to move them.

__External validation for outcome claims.__ A standing research question that asks whether the DGENIE model itself produces a claimed outcome, not merely whether it was implemented as designed, cannot be answered solely from evidence the model's own Mentors, rubrics, and certifications produced; a system cannot be the sole judge of whether it works. For any such question, at minimum the Genius Cycle outcome question named in Section Four, the Chief Educational Architect commissions an external evaluator with no employment, financial, or reporting relationship to DGENIE to independently assess a sample of Genius Portfolio artifacts against the published rubric, blinded to the artifact's existing Mentor certification and Genius Level, at least once every three years. Where the external evaluator's independent judgment diverges materially from DGENIE's own certification record, that divergence is reported through the same public-accountability channel named in Section Eleven, not adjusted away, and is treated as a live finding requiring the Institutional Learning Loop's attention under Section Ten.

## SECTION TWO — PURPOSE & SCOPE

Blueprint 04 names what evidence DGENIE gathers, and who certifies what that evidence proves. No document in the Charter defines how multiple, sometimes disagreeing, pieces of evidence about the same learner become one coherent statement about their development. This Specification is that missing method.

It applies wherever more than one evidence stream, defined in Section Three, bears on the same question about a learner: readiness for a Genius Level advancement, a Masterpiece Project milestone, a Transition Conversation under the Learner Transition & Life Navigation content in Blueprint 04, or any moment a mentor must form a judgment about a learner from more than a single source.

It does not apply to a single-source observation a mentor is confident stands on its own. Triangulation is required where evidence streams could plausibly disagree, not imposed as bureaucratic overhead on every routine observation.

---

## SECTION THREE — THE FOUR EVIDENCE STREAMS

Every evidence source named in Blueprint 04's Assessment Philosophy resolves into one of four streams for the purpose of triangulation.

__Self-report.__ The learner's own account: student reflection, and what a learner says about themselves in conversation, in a Genie Check-In or Check-Out, or directly to a mentor.

__AI-observed pattern.__ What the Assessment Assistant surfaces from the Genius Portfolio against the rubric standards established in Blueprint 04, and what My Genie's continuity record, per Blueprint 02, shows about a learner's recurring behavior across sessions. This stream is pattern, not judgment, per Section Six.

__Human observation.__ Teacher observations, mentor feedback, and parent feedback, gathered by the adults in a learner's Relationship Network per Blueprint 08.

__Artifact evidence.__ Authentic projects, presentations, portfolios, practical demonstrations, and written examinations: the tangible, reviewable record a learner produced.

A single piece of evidence may inform more than one stream. A Masterpiece Project presentation is both artifact evidence and, through the questions a learner answers about their own work, self-report.

---

## SECTION FOUR — THE TRIANGULATION METHOD

Where two or more streams bear on the same question, a mentor synthesizes them through the following four steps, worked here against a representative example.

__Step one — state the question.__ What is actually being assessed. Example: does this learner show emerging leadership capability.

__Step two — gather each stream without collapsing them early.__ Self-report: the learner says, "I love leadership." AI-observed pattern: My Genie's continuity record shows the learner frequently initiates collaborative activities. Human observation: a mentor notes strong initiative but weak listening. Artifact evidence: the Genius Portfolio shows three completed, mentor-certified led projects.

__Step three — look for convergence and divergence, not agreement or disagreement.__ These four streams do not contradict each other; they describe different facets of the same developing capability. Convergence: initiative appears in self-report, AI-observed pattern, and artifact evidence alike. Divergence: human observation adds a specific limitation, weak listening, that no other stream surfaced.

__Step four — synthesize, do not average.__ The conclusion is not the loudest single stream, and it is not a blended score. It names both what converges and what a single stream alone caught. For this example: *emerging leadership capability, with demonstrated initiative and a development need in active listening.* This is the standard every triangulation under this Specification is held to: specific enough to act on, honest enough to include the limitation, and never collapsed into a flat label like "future leader" that a single stream might have produced alone.

---

## SECTION FIVE — SYNTHESIS OUTPUTS AND WHERE THEY LIVE

A completed triangulation is recorded in the Genius Portfolio as a synthesis statement, attributed to the mentor who formed it and dated, alongside the individual evidence streams it drew on, which remain visible rather than being discarded once synthesized.

A synthesis statement informs, and where relevant directly supports, a Genius Level certification decision under Blueprint 04's four-tier rubric, a Masterpiece Project milestone, or a Transition Conversation under Blueprint 04's Learner Transition & Life Navigation content. It is evidence for that decision. It is not, by itself, the decision, which remains a human mentor's act of certification per Blueprint 04.

## SECTION SIX — THE AI BOUNDARY: GATHERS EVIDENCE, NEVER DECIDES

Blueprint 02 states plainly that Artificial Intelligence shall never determine a learner's identity, purpose, calling, or destiny. Blueprint 14 Part One's Agent Four boundary states that only a human mentor certifies a Genius Level advancement. This Section applies both, specifically and without exception, to every triangulation performed under this Specification.

The Assessment Assistant and My Genie may populate Section Four's evidence streams. Step three's convergence-and-divergence read and step four's synthesis, the interpretive acts of this method, are performed by a human mentor. An AI agent may draft a proposed synthesis statement for a mentor's review, consistent with the Assessment Assistant's stated function of generating assessment summaries, but a draft is not a synthesis until a human mentor has reviewed and adopted it as their own judgment.

Where a learner, parent, or mentor asks whether a synthesis statement came from a human or an AI draft a human adopted, that distinction is disclosed plainly, per the right to transparent AI established in Blueprint 07 Part Two.

## SECTION SEVEN — DIVERGENT AND CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE

Section Four's worked example showed streams that diverge without contradicting. This Section governs the harder case: streams that genuinely conflict, a self-report and a human observation that cannot both be straightforwardly true, or an AI-observed pattern that a mentor's own direct experience of the learner does not recognize.

Where evidence streams genuinely conflict, the mentor does not force a synthesis. Consistent with the precedent set in Blueprint 04's stalled-learner investigation, the mentor seeks a second opinion from another mentor before recording a synthesis statement, and the conflict itself, not only its eventual resolution, is recorded in the Portfolio as part of an honest developmental record.

A contradiction is never resolved by defaulting to the AI-observed stream over the human streams, or the reverse. It is resolved by a second human judgment, applied to all four streams together.

A related but distinct case, added 2026-08-14: a Mentor's own uncertainty about a domain-specific, qualitative rubric bar, per Blueprint 04 Part Thirteen. This is not streams genuinely conflicting; it is one Mentor's confidence in applying a bar, such as whether a learner has used a domain's vocabulary correctly, running out because that Mentor was trained broadly rather than as a specialist in that particular domain. The same second-opinion mechanism applies, with one difference in who is consulted: where the uncertainty is specifically about clearing a domain's qualitative bar rather than about which of two conflicting accounts is true, the second opinion is sought from whoever actually holds relevant domain background, another Mentor, a Curriculum Architect resource, or a screened Community Expert, not simply the nearest available Mentor. As with any second opinion under this Section, the certifying Mentor's authority to certify is not transferred by the consultation; their confidence in exercising it is.

__A third case, added 2026-08-15, that neither of the above two covers: nobody, including the Mentor, has to notice a problem before it is checked.__ Both cases above are Mentor-initiated: a Mentor recognizes conflicting streams, or recognizes their own uncertainty, and asks for a second opinion. That leaves the harder failure mode this session's structural audit named directly: a Mentor whose caseload has grown past what sustains careful judgment does not typically notice their own drift, they simply certify a little more generously, and a mechanism that only activates when the Mentor asks for it never catches a Mentor who, under strain, stops asking. The check for this case cannot be Mentor-initiated by design. Instead, the __Educational Audits__ described in Blueprint 08 Part One, Section Ten, already conducted by the Chief Educational Architect on a recurring cycle, include a sampled review of certified Genius Level advancements, drawn without the certifying Mentor's foreknowledge of which will be sampled, re-triangulated against Section Four's four-stream method by someone other than the original certifying Mentor. A pattern of certifications that would not have cleared the same rubric bar under independent re-triangulation is a finding reported through the Educational Audit, not a private correction to the individual Mentor alone, consistent with this Section's existing principle that a contradiction is resolved by a second human judgment, never by deference to whichever judgment came first.

A Genius Level advancement, per Blueprint 04, is certified against a four-tier rubric bar using a portfolio artifact, a mentor observation, a practical demonstration, or a combination. Where a combination is used, that combination is a triangulation under this Specification, and the synthesis statement produced in Section Four becomes part of the evidence record the four-tier rubric is checked against.

This Specification does not lower or raise the rubric bar itself, which remains the Assessment Architect's responsibility per Blueprint 08, guarding against rubric drift or grade inflation. It ensures the evidence reaching that bar has been honestly combined rather than cherry-picked from whichever single stream supports the desired outcome.

## SECTION TEN — RECORDING AND AUDIT TRAIL

Every synthesis statement is recorded with its date, the mentor who formed it, the individual evidence streams considered, and, where Section Seven applied, the second mentor consulted. This record is part of the Genius Portfolio's defensible account of growth, per Blueprint 04, and is available to the learner and, where the learner is a minor, their parent, per the transparency and visibility standards already established in Blueprint 08 and Blueprint 12.

Access alone is not the same as a felt experience of one's own growth. Where a learner or parent views this record, the presentation is built to be read as a growth story, not a data table: the learner's own evidence and certified synthesis statements set against their own prior record, at the cadence of the Season structure established in Blueprint 04, never against another learner's, consistent with the Educational Red Lines established in Blueprint 04. Where a piece of evidence shown has been surfaced by the Assessment Assistant or My Genie but not yet reviewed and adopted by a mentor per Section Six, it is labeled plainly as an observation, not a verdict, consistent with the Mirror Principle established in Blueprint 02. This does not create a new AI boundary; it applies the one already established in Section Six to what a learner sees, not only to what a mentor certifies.

## CONSTITUTIONAL DEFINITIONS

__Standing research question.__ One of the questions named in Part One, Section Four, or a question later added to that list under Part One, Section Twelve, testing whether a specific part of the Charter's design is producing its intended outcome in practice.

__Institutional Learning Loop.__ The process, defined in Part One, Section Ten, by which a published research finding reaches the constitutional channel with authority to act on it, without itself holding that authority.

__Research consent.__ The specific, explicit family consent required under Part One, Section Six before any longitudinal or individually-traceable data is used in research, distinct from general consent to ordinary platform operation.

__Evidence stream.__ One of the four categories, defined in Part Two, Section Three, that every evidence source named in Blueprint 04's Assessment Philosophy resolves into: self-report, AI-observed pattern, human observation, or artifact evidence.

__Triangulation.__ The four-step method, defined in Part Two, Section Four, by which a mentor combines two or more evidence streams into a synthesis statement.

__Synthesis statement.__ The specific, honest, non-averaged conclusion produced by triangulation, recorded in the Genius Portfolio per Part Two, Section Five.

__Convergence.__ Where two or more evidence streams independently point to the same conclusion, per Part Two, Section Four, Step Three.

__Divergence.__ Where evidence streams describe different, non-contradictory facets of the same question, per Part Two, Section Four, Step Three, distinct from contradiction, governed by Part Two, Section Seven.
